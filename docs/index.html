asal

asal is a tool built for one small export business that sells legal goods from Iran to Iraq, including cosmetics (a collagen hydrating serum) and disposable hospital textiles.

The business uses TikTok to show its products. asal is meant to make that work easier in two simple ways:

  1. Help publish the business's own product videos to its own TikTok account.
  2. Show the account's basic profile information and its public videos.

How it works

asal does not work without permission. The TikTok account owner must first log in inside TikTok and approve the connection. The owner can disconnect the tool at any time.

asal is not a service for the general public. It has no other users, and it does not offer accounts to anyone else.
