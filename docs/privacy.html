Privacy Policy — asal

Last updated: October 23, 2026

What this tool can see

After the TikTok account owner gives permission inside TikTok, this tool can see only:

  • Basic profile information (such as display name, username, avatar, and basic account statistics)
  • The account's public videos and their basic information

What this tool does NOT collect

  • It does not collect or store the user's TikTok password. Login always happens inside TikTok.
  • It does not read, collect, or send direct messages (private messages).
  • It does not access private account content beyond what the owner has approved through TikTok.

How the data is used

The data above is used only to run the tool: to show the account's profile and public videos to the owner, and to publish the business's own product videos to the owner's account after permission.

No sale of data

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share TikTok account data with third parties for marketing.

Keeping the data

Access continues only while the owner keeps the tool connected. The owner can disconnect the tool at any time from inside TikTok, which ends its access.
